A dance class made for adults

Our students range from their 20s to their 70s, and most have never danced before.

Any age, any fitness level

Tango is built on walking and listening rather than stamina or complex choreography, so it suits every age and fitness level. What matters is your enthusiasm to learn, not your age.

No choreography to memorise

You learn to walk together to the music, step by step. We break every movement into small steps, with the focus on clear communication and the music.

Relaxed, in small groups

No pressure, just fun: mistakes are part of learning and everyone starts as a beginner. We keep groups small on purpose so everyone gets personal attention.

Teaching since 2007

BE-TANGO has been teaching Argentine tango in Brussels since 2007. Classes are taught in French and English, and we also speak Dutch.

The beginner cycle

12 one-hour lessons + 1 practica for €195. A new cycle starts every September and February.

Lessons 1–4: the fundamentals

Posture, balance and the tango walk. You learn to walk forwards and backwards with confidence and connection, and find the embrace that feels right for you.

Lessons 5–8: ochos and turns

You learn the basic ocho (the figure-eight step) and how to lead and follow simple turns, always with the focus on the music.

Lessons 9–12: putting it together

You combine what you have learned into short sequences and learn to navigate a busy dance floor. By the end you will feel ready for your first milonga in Brussels.

What You'll Learn

Foundation & Posture

The correct posture and embrace (abrazo) that forms the foundation of Argentine tango, with a focus on comfort, connection and body awareness.

Walking & Basic Steps

The fundamental tango walk and basic step patterns, so you move with confidence, balance and musicality.

Partner Connection

The connection between leader and follower: communicating through the embrace, a dialogue without words.

Musicality & Milonga

How tango music is structured, the difference between tango, vals and milonga, and milonga etiquette so you can join the social tango community with confidence.

Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need a dance partner?

+

No. Most of our students come alone. If you sign up without a partner, we match you with a fixed dance partner for the full course. Your registration is confirmed once we find the right match.

Do I need any previous dance experience?

+

Absolutely not. Our beginner classes are designed for people with zero dance experience. We start from the very basics and progress step by step. If you can walk, you can learn tango.

Am I too old to start?

+

No. Our students range from their 20s to their 70s. Tango is for everyone: what matters is your enthusiasm to learn, not your age.

How much does the course cost?

+

Your first class is free, with no obligation. The full beginner cycle (12 lessons + 1 practica) costs €195.

What should I wear and bring?

+

Wear comfortable clothes that allow you to move freely. For shoes, any comfortable shoes with leather or smooth soles work for beginners. Avoid sneakers with rubber soles as they can stick to the floor.

What language are the classes taught in?

+

Classes are taught in French and English. We also speak Dutch, so you can get explanations in all three languages.

When do the courses start?

+

New beginner cycles start twice a year, in September and February. You can try a free trial class first to experience our teaching style.
